🚀 Quick Guide for Kids (No hard words)

🌱 Learn how we choose your lunar camp in 4 ideas
1. Circling Sun ☀️ The Sun is always low At the South Pole the Sun goes around like a lighthouse.
💡 On top of the mountains it almost never goes out.
2. Best Days 🗓️ Days in a row without shadows We look for the run of days with the most light and signal.
💡 The golden box in the drawing marks the winning days.
3. Calls 📱 Talking to your family If a mountain hides the Earth, we use a satellite.
💡 On Malapert the call almost never drops.
4. Lunar Ice 🧊 Very cold frozen water The Sun never reaches the bottom of the craters.
💡 Try Cabeus crater if you want to look for ice.
